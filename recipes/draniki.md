# Draniki

Belarusian potato pancakes. Serves 4, takes about 40 minutes.

## Ingredients
- 1 kg potatoes
- 1 onion
- 1 egg
- 2 tbsp wheat flour
- 1 tsp salt
- Ground black pepper
- Sunflower oil for frying
- Sour cream for serving

## Instructions
1. Peel the potatoes and the onion.
2. Grate both on the fine side of a grater into one bowl. The onion juice keeps the potatoes from turning grey.
3. Put the mixture into a sieve and press out as much liquid as you can.
4. Return it to the bowl and mix in the egg, flour, salt and a pinch of pepper.
5. Heat about 3 mm of oil in a frying pan over medium heat.
6. Spoon in the batter, one heaped tablespoon per pancake, and flatten each one slightly.
7. Fry for 3-4 minutes on each side until golden brown.
8. Drain on paper towels and serve hot with sour cream.
