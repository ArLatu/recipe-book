# Syrniki

Fried cottage cheese pancakes. Makes 10-12 pieces, takes about 30 minutes.

## Ingredients
- 500 g firm cottage cheese (kohupiim)
- 1 egg
- 3-4 tbsp wheat flour, plus extra for coating
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla sugar
- A pinch of salt
- Sunflower oil or butter for frying
- Sour cream or jam for serving

## Instructions
1. Mash the cottage cheese with a fork until no large lumps remain.
2. Add the egg, sugar, vanilla sugar and salt, and mix well.
3. Add the flour one spoon at a time until the dough is soft but holds its shape.
4. With floured hands, shape 10-12 round patties about 1.5 cm thick and coat them lightly in flour.
5. Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium-low heat.
6. Fry the syrniki for 3-4 minutes on each side until golden brown. To check that they are cooked inside, press the middle lightly: it should feel firm and spring back. If it is still soft, cover the pan and cook for 2 more minutes on low heat.
7. Serve warm with sour cream or jam.
