# Kartulisalat

Estonian potato salad. Serves 6, takes about 1 hour plus cooling time.

## Ingredients
- 600 g potatoes
- 4 eggs
- 3 pickled cucumbers
- 250 g ham or boiled sausage
- 1 small onion or 3 spring onions
- 150 g sour cream
- 100 g mayonnaise
- 1 tsp mustard
- Salt and ground black pepper

## Instructions
1. Boil the potatoes in their skins until tender, about 20 minutes.
2. Hard-boil the eggs for 10 minutes, then cool them in cold water.
3. Let the potatoes cool completely, then peel the potatoes and the eggs.
4. Cut the potatoes, eggs, cucumbers and ham into cubes of about 1 cm.
5. Chop the onion finely.
6. In a separate bowl, stir together the sour cream, mayonnaise and mustard.
7. Combine everything, season with salt and pepper and mix gently.
8. Chill in the fridge for at least 1 hour before serving.
